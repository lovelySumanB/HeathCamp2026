Clinical Scope

Available Medical Services

Comprehensive health screenings conducted by licensed doctors, nurses, and lab technicians using state-of-the-art equipment.

General Health Checkup

Full physical examination including respiratory rate, pulse, temperature, and general systemic assessment.

Basic & Preventive

Blood Pressure Check

Digital & manual sphygmomanometer readings for early detection of hypertension and cardiac anomalies.

Cardiovascular

Blood Sugar Test

Instant glucometer testing for fasting and random blood sugar to detect pre-diabetes and diabetes.

Endocrine

BMI & Vitals Check

Body Mass Index measurement, waist-hip ratio evaluation, and body fat composition screening.

Metabolic

Eye Checkup

Visual acuity test, refraction error evaluation, cataract detection, and eye health consultation.

Ophthalmology

Dental Checkup

Oral hygiene evaluation, cavity check, gum health inspection, and free preventive hygiene kits.

Oral Health

Nutrition Consultation

Personalized diet counseling, micronutrient guidance, and lifestyle modifications for chronic disease control.

Dietetics

Doctor Consultation

One-on-one confidential consultation with senior general physicians and medical specialists.

Why Attend?

Dedicated to Promoting Community Wellness & Early Detection

Regular health checks are essential for identifying risk factors before they turn into major health complications. Our camp brings qualified doctors and clinical diagnostics directly to your neighborhood.

Zero Cost Consultations & Tests

Every screening, consultation, and diagnostic report is provided completely free of charge.

Instant Diagnostic Reports

Receive your blood pressure, sugar, and vital reports immediately along with doctor notes.

Private & Confidential

Your medical consultations and details are kept strictly private under medical standards.
